## Usage

Use `variant` to choose the visual treatment:

- `variant="default"` - the standard unboxed expandable
- `variant="box"` - the boxed expandable treatment
- `variant="box-bleed"` - the boxed treatment that bleeds to the container edge on small screens
- `variant="default-with-divider"` - the full-width row treatment with dividers

New usage should prefer `variant`. The `box` and `bleed` flags are deprecated and remain supported only for compatibility.
